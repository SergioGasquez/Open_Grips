Choose, drop, or paste a hand photo

1. Photo

Palm up, fingers straight, ruler at palm height beside the fingers, camera parallel to the table. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, drag a point to move it, click a step to place it again.

2. Points

Click the four circled points on every finger. The segments and how far each base crease sits along the hand place the roller (Height, Depth); DIP → tip sets the blocker (To_Blocker).

Pick two ruler marks far apart that span the fingers (e.g. 0 and 10 cm), not the palm: camera tilt changes the scale across the photo. If a joint has a double crease, click midway between the creases.
